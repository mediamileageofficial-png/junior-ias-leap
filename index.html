The Programme

What is LEAP?

LEAP is a one-year Leadership Discovery Programme by Junior IAS. It introduces children to the habits, skills and mindset that help them grow into confident, responsible and capable individuals — long before competitive exams begin.

L

Leadership

Speaking up, taking initiative and guiding others with confidence.

E

Excellence

Strong basics and steady habits that make learning feel light, not heavy.

A

Awareness

Understanding people, society and the world around them.

P

Purpose

Curiosity and direction — discovering potential before choosing a path.

Meet Your Mentor

Anandha Reshmi

Founder & IAS Mentor · WE4U IAS

Since her school days, Anandha Reshmi wanted to become an IAS officer. She prepared for the UPSC examination — but without the right guidance at the right time, the journey was far harder than it needed to be.

Today she mentors young students so they never face that gap. Her belief is simple: a long-term goal, set early and guided well, changes everything — and that conviction is exactly what LEAP is built on.

“நீங்கள் IAS ஆக வேண்டுமா? இதற்கு long-term goal மிக முக்கியம்.” “Do you want to become an IAS officer? For that, a long-term goal matters most.” — Anandha Reshmi
